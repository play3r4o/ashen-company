## Usage rules

- Outlines are colored darks, not universal pure black.
- Burgundy identifies the company through small cloth, wax, trim and UI-state accents.
- Amber is local light and emphasis, not a global yellow wash.
- Verdigris is rare and narratively supernatural. Mundane poison uses sick olive instead.
- Highlights favor warm linen or desaturated material colors; avoid white specular dots everywhere.
- Adjacent materials separate by value as well as hue.

## Color-count targets

- Small props and icons: 8–16 colors.
- Ordinary characters: 16–28 colors.
- Buildings: 24–40 colors across all materials.
- Terrain material family: 8–20 colors.
- Effects: 5–12 colors plus documented partial alpha if necessary.

Additional colors must create a visible material, lighting or readability benefit.

## UI tokens

- Panel cavity: outline plum-navy and peat darks.
- Frame: dark oak and iron.
- Primary copy: light linen.
- Secondary copy: muted linen.
- Numeric/stat emphasis: amber.
- Selected/active: burgundy plus shape change.
- Supernatural warning: verdigris plus icon/shape change.
- Destructive action: dark oxblood, not saturated arcade red.

## Drift rejection

Reject bright cyan-and-cream heroic fantasy, faction color covering most surfaces, hue without value separation, unrelated pastel ramps, or another pack's palette relationships reproduced with slightly different hex values.
